## 2. 还能说的（每条都有边界）

### 主张 A ⭐ 把力学放回 grammar

**原句级差异。** Fang et al. 自己写明：

> "Kinematic grammar describes human body movements **without considering forces**."

### 主张 B ⭐ 离散符号词表承载 grammar

- Gu et al. 做了符号化（手部舞谱），Fang et al. 做了 grammar（BRNN），**没人把两者合起来，且都不是上肢临床**。
- ✅ 可写："a **discrete symbolic vocabulary** on which biomechanical constraints are enforced"
- 🆕 **2026-10-03 补第三条论证轴：「为什么要离散」的答案不止压缩与可解释性**
  —— 来自 **E12（Cleffken 2007, J Shoulder Elbow Surg 16(6):788–794，`10.1016/j.jse.2007.02.134`）**：
  肘关节的**测试者重测 SDD 为 4.2°–10.8°**（主动 ROM **8.5°**、被动 ROM **10.8°**；
  Bland–Altman 修正法，42 人 × 144 次，电子量角器，双盲 test–retest）。
  → **论证链**：① 在 ±5–10° 的测量噪声下，**连续角度输出在该带内不可解释**（「137.3°」的数位精度是假的）；
  ② **锚定在 ROM 边界上的符号**能给出**在噪声下仍成立的陈述**（「接近全伸」）；
  ③ **输出粒度必须与测量精度匹配**（*output granularity must match measurement precision*）。
  📌 **这条与既有工作正交**：DigitCode 的离散化目标是 **rate–distortion（压缩）**，
  「可解释性」是记谱传统**已有的**原则（F30），**而「匹配测量精度」目前没人这样论证过** ——
  且它给 `ViKiT_方法概览.md` §2.4 **判据 ④** 补上了**第二条独立依据**（第一条是 F24）。
  🚨 **三条限制必须一起写，否则会被一句 "SDD ≠ accuracy" 驳回**：
  ① **SDD 是 precision 不是 accuracy**（是 accuracy 的一个分量）；
  ② **量角器不是实验室级参照**（光学动捕可重复性远好于此）→ **8.5° 是「临床参照标准的下限」，不是「视觉方法的下限」**；
  ③ 🚨 **仅肘关节** —— 肩与腕**未找到同规格 SDD**。
  📌 **这同时改了一条实验设计约束**：**不得以人工量角器为参照声称 5° 级精度**
  —— 参照标准自己就在 8.5° 波动，此时报出的差异无法归因于算法。详见 V7 §1.12。
- ⚠️ 边界：**「Kinematic Tokenization」这个方法名已被占用**（`2601.09949`，金融领域）——别直接用。
- 🚨 **2026-10-01 补充边界（第二代追溯）**：
  - **离散姿态范式本身已被占**：VQ-HPS（ECCV 2024，`2312.08291`）已把「离散隐表征 + 分类任务」用在人体网格上，并自称 novel paradigm。
    → 差异必须落在**词表的可解释性与物理约束**上，不是落在「我们也做了离散化」。
    → **可用的真差异**：VQ-HPS 的码本是**无监督学出来的、不可解释、不可编辑**；
    我们的词表**有语法、有 ROM 边界、可人工检查**。
  - **"tokenizer" 一词已被占**：Hourglass Tokenizer（CVPR 2024，`2311.12028`）在 Transformer 姿态估计里用了这个词。
    → 但**两者同形异义**：HoT 剪的是**输入侧时间冗余帧**（为省 FLOPs）；我们要的是**输出侧符号量化**（为语法约束）。
    → 📌 **建议 `03_方法设计/Anatomy_aware_Region_Encoder.md` 改名** codebook / vocabulary / symbol table，避免评审直接撞脸（登记 G8）。

| 5 | *"**Data reduction** (on the order of **1/3** of 3D keypoint data)"* | 量化的离散化收益 |

**第 4 条为什么是关键**：一个符号/token 只有在**能独立指定**时才有意义。
原文明说「**每个关节的角度独立于其他关节的角度**」——
**这正是「一个符号 = 一个关节的一个量」这种词表设计能成立的依据。**
📌 **所以主张 D 的答案不再是「我们选关节角」，而是**：
「**关节角是唯一被独立解耦的人体运动学量，因此它是离散符号词表唯一自然的原子单位**」——
**这句话现在有 A 级可引证的出处。**

**✅ 主张 D 现在的完整形态**：
- **引述** Gu et al. 的问题（符号覆盖骨/指/整手？）—— 把撞车变成对话；
- **正面回答**：原子单位 = **ISB 定义的关节角**（不是骨段、不是像素、不是隐向量）；
- **论证**：ISB 角度**逐关节独立可指定**（Schlegel 第 4 条）→ 天然可做 token；
- **衔接**：这同时**回答了 V6c 的一部分** —— 旋转该挂在哪个关节，
  取决于该关节在 ISB 里被定义成几个 DoF（腕 3 / 肘 2 / 肩 3，语料三 B1 已有 Part II）。
  ⚠️ **但 V6c 仍需你裁定**：上面给的是**单位选择原则**，不是**具体挂载方案**。

## 3. 定位陈述草稿（Related Work / Introduction 可直接改写）

> Markerless motion capture has increasingly incorporated biomechanical structure.
> Recently, a *pose grammar* was proposed that encodes human body configuration —
> kinematic, symmetry and motor-coordination constraints — as a hierarchy of bidirectional
> RNNs [Fang et al., AAAI 2018], and this line has since been extended to data augmentation
> [Xu et al., TPAMI 2022] and direction-aware grammar [Zhou et al., TBME 2023].
> The original formulation is **explicitly defined without considering forces** [Fang et al.,
> AAAI 2018]; its successors retain that exclusion nowhere in their own statements — Xu et al. instead
> state that they *"explicitly incorporate a set of knowledge regarding human body configuration
> (i.e., kinematics, symmetry and motor coordination)"*. What all of them share is representational:
> the grammar is realized as **network modules over continuous joint states — a hierarchy of
> bidirectional RNNs, then multi-scale 3D convolutions** — so the output remains a **continuous
> pose estimate, with no codebook, no symbol table, no token granularity, and nothing a human can
> read**. This is the gap we occupy: the grammar must be carried by a **discrete kinematic vocabulary**.
>
> Two further lines must be positioned against explicitly. First, **discrete representations
> of human pose already exist**: Hand Labanotation has been used as an alphabet for symbolic
> hand-motion tokenization [Gu et al., 2026], and VQ-HPS [Fiche et al., ECCV 2024] frames
> human pose and shape estimation as **classification over a vector-quantized latent space**.
> Crucially, VQ-HPS's codebook is **learned without biomechanical structure** — its symbols
> carry no anatomical meaning and cannot be inspected or edited.
>
> Second, the idea that human movement admits a **discrete notation** is far from new.
> Labanotation, formulated in 1928, has been described explicitly as *"a universal movement
> notation language"* [Barbacci, 2002] and was published in the physical-therapy literature
> as early as 1954. Most recently, Li et al. [ACM MM 2024] built a **Hand Labanotation**
> dataset of 4M annotated images and a **multi-view transformer that translates hand
> movements into that notation**, arguing that *"data-oriented representations, e.g.,
> coordinates of hand keypoints, are not intuitive and vulnerable to noise."* We share that
> argument, and we therefore do not claim it. **The difference is one of target and of
> discipline**: their notation serves artistic documentation and robotic-hand control, its
> symbols are kinematic only, and its citation network remains in dance, martial arts and
> cultural heritage — **it has never been used to estimate clinically meaningful
> upper-limb joint angles.**
>
> **The two lines have not been combined**, and neither addresses the **upper limb** or
> **clinical rehabilitation**. We close this gap by formulating upper-limb kinematics as a
> **structured Seq2Seq translation** problem: a visual Transformer maps image features to a
> **discrete kinematic vocabulary that is itself biomechanically structured** — bounded by
> ranges of motion, coupled by joint constraints, and human-inspectable — on which a
> **biomechanical grammar**, *including a dynamics-consistency term that the original formulation
> [Fang et al., AAAI 2018] explicitly excludes*, is enforced, jointly with a second musculoskeletal
> output head.
>
> The vocabulary is **structured for the full ISB three-DoF wrist**, whose angles are the
> quantities clinicians and movement scientists already use to communicate posture and motion
> [Schlegel et al., 2024]. Forearm pronation/supination, however, is **not available in public
> motion-capture datasets** — and it was **not ranked among the metrics clinicians considered
> worth measuring**, being dropped from the final seventeen kinematic metrics in a recent
> multi-centre consensus study [Pohl et al., 2026, Table S2]. We therefore **leave the third
> wrist slot explicitly unfilled and documented**, rather than silently reducing the wrist to
> two degrees of freedom: the structure states where the quantity belongs, and a study with
> instrumented forearm rotation can populate it without redesigning the vocabulary.

> A third line constrains the problem itself. For upper-limb rotation in particular,
> transformer-based monocular methods recover arm and hand dynamics [Liu et al., CVPR 2022],
> yet that work states that **"the twist component cannot be determined by joint locations"**,
> evaluates only a lumped arm-rotation error, and sets wrist rotations to zero when comparing
> rotations. Forearm pronation/supination is thus both the least measured and the least
> identifiable quantity in this setting, which is precisely why our vocabulary is
> **biomechanically structured** rather than learned: the symbols must obey the coupling that
> makes twist recoverable at all.
>
> The clinical motivation is interpretability, not accuracy — but it is a motivation with a
> history. The clinical community once had exactly this: **Benesh Movement Notation was used
> from the 1970s to the 1990s as a recording tool for rehabilitation outcome and for
> clinical communication** [Harrison, 1985; Harrison et al., 1992], and Labanotation itself
> appeared in the physical-therapy literature as early as 1954. That tradition was manual,
> whole-body and did not survive into the 2000s. Meanwhile a focus-group study with 11
> neurorehabilitation clinicians [Pohl et al., 2026] finds that practitioners still rely
> largely on *"simply looking"* at movement, judge quality by **smoothness, efficiency and
> compensatory patterns**, and are notably **unfamiliar with jerk and inter-joint
> coordination**. A vocabulary whose symbols are physically meaningful and human-inspectable
> is therefore not a self-evident good: the clinical attempt at it has already failed once.
> We accordingly surface **clinically used quantities** — range of motion, symmetry, movement
> timing, compensation — alongside the grammar-level tokens they constrain, so that the
> symbolic layer is a constraint on the representation rather than a replacement for the
> measures clinicians already read.
>
> Forearm pronation/supination is the one ISB wrist degree of freedom that our vocabulary
> reserves but does not fill, and we state this openly rather than as a contribution. Two
> independent lines of evidence motivate the reservation. On the clinical side, a focus-group

> collapsed two-dimensional wrist.

### 10.7 第二十轮：V7d 闭合，并出现本项目**同向独立来源最多**的一条链

**这一轮回答了一个一直挂着的问题：上肢到底有没有自己的临床误差阈值。**
答案是**有，而且比从下肢借来的 5° 宽得多**。

#### 找到的三个新证据（全部 Crossref + Europe PMC 双源反查）

| 证据 | 部位 | 类型 | 关键数值/结论 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cleffken 2007**（`10.1016/j.jse.2007.02.134`）| **肘** | 测量者重测 **SDD**（Bland–Altman 修正法，42 人 × 144 次，电子量角器，双盲）| 主动 ROM **0±8.5°**、被动 ROM **0±10.8°**（单项 4.2–10.1°）|
| **Alford 2021**（`10.1016/j.jht.2020.05.001`）| **腕 + 前臂旋转** | **ICC** 分层（40 人 / 80 腕）| 腕屈伸 **good** > 前臂旋转 **moderate** > **旋前自测 poor** |
| **Antonietti 2014**（`10.5935/medicalexpress.2014.02.08`）| **肩** | 内外旋 ROM 可靠性 | ⚠️ **无摘要、数值不可得** → 已列 **P0-8**，**未采用** |

#### ⭐ 四条同向、类型互不重叠的独立来源

| # | 来源 | 类型 | 说的是什么 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | TMRB 2026 | **CV 基准** | 前臂旋转**零结果**（没报） |
| 2 | Pohl 2026 表 S2 | **临床调查** | 临床清单**排除了它**（不想测） |
| 3 | Cleffken 2007 | **测量者重测 SDD** | 肘 ROM 自身就波动 **8.5°**（难测准） |
| 4 | Alford 2021 | **自测可靠性** | **旋前是最差的一个**（poor） |

📌 **四者来源类型互不重叠**（基准 / 调查 / 重测 / 自测），**方向完全一致** ——
**这是本项目目前最硬的一条链**，且它不依赖任何「检索未命中」。

#### 🚨 三条必须一起写的边界（否则就是又一次外推）

| # | 边界 |
|---|---|
| 1 | **SDD ≠ ICC**（绝对差异 vs 相对一致性）—— **两者数值不可并列，只能比序数方向** |
| 2 | 🚫 **Alford 不复活主张 C** —— F29 已证伪「没人报过旋前旋后精度」；Alford 讲的是**「测的时候多不可靠」**，不是**「有没有人测过」**。**两个不同命题。** |
| 3 | 🚫 **「poor」只出现在自测条件**；检查者测量时前臂旋转是 **moderate**。**不得写成「旋前测不准」。** |

#### 📌 两条落地的后果

**① 实验设计约束**：🚫 **不得以人工量角器为参照声称 5° 级精度** ——
参照标准自身重测就波动 8.5°，此时报出的差异**无法归因于算法**。
**应优先用光学动捕做主验证，量角器只作临床可比性的次要验证。**

**② 给「为什么要离散」补了第三条论证轴**（见主张 B）：
在 ±5–10° 的测量噪声下，**连续角度输出在该带内不可解释**；
**ROM 锚定的符号**能给出**噪声下仍成立**的陈述。
→ **输出粒度必须与测量精度匹配**（*output granularity must match measurement precision*）。
📌 **这条与 DigitCode 的率–失真（压缩）轴正交**，与 F30 的「可解释性」（记谱传统已有）也不同 ——
它给 `ViKiT_方法概览.md` §2.4 **判据 ④** 补上了**第二条独立依据**。
